About

I'm a computer scientist and educator based in Vancouver, British Columbia. Since December 2023 I've been a faculty member in Computer Information Technology at the British Columbia Institute of Technology, where I teach and lead the Industry Sponsored Student Projects capstone course at the Downtown Campus.

Before teaching, I spent seven years building software across R&D, full-stack product development, and technical leadership. I worked on early prototypes, data-rich web applications, and production systems, often in close collaboration with UX and product teams.

My graduate work lives on both sides of the human–machine line: a master's in machine learning and robotics at the University of Tehran, where an air-hockey robot was the testbed for learning from sparse demonstrations; and a master's in human–computer interaction at the University of Calgary, where I built systems that let people dance and exercise together across a distance.

I teach in BCIT’s Computer Information Technology department. In Fall 2026, I’m teaching Programming Methods and leading the Industry Sponsored Student Projects capstone course at the Downtown Campus.

Industry experience

Across seven years in software engineering, I worked from early research and rough prototypes through production delivery and operations. The domains changed, but the recurring work was making ambiguous problems concrete alongside design, product, and engineering teams.

Data products and visualization

Full-stack product development
