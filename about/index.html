About

I'm a computer scientist and educator based in Vancouver, British Columbia. Since December 2023 I've been a faculty member in Computer Information Technology at the British Columbia Institute of Technology, where I teach and lead the Industry Sponsored Student Projects capstone program.

Before teaching, I spent close to a decade building software: a payments integration surface for Microsoft Teams, economic-development data products at Localintel, game-based learning activities at SMART Technologies, and real-time monitoring front-ends at Guavus. In between, a short, intense stretch leading engineering at a pre-launch startup called Replayz.

My graduate work lives on both sides of the human–machine line: a master's in machine learning and robotics at the University of Tehran, where an air-hockey robot was the testbed for learning from sparse demonstrations; and a master's in human–computer interaction at the University of Calgary, where I built systems that let people dance and exercise together across a distance.

Teaching goes back a while: I was a teaching assistant at the University of Calgary (human–computer interaction, introductory computer science) and at the University of Tehran (artificial intelligence, social network analysis), and earlier I taught programming at Allameh Helli No. 3 high school in Tehran — where, with colleagues, I co-authored a C++ textbook for high-school students.
