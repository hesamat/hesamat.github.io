About Hesam

About me

I’m an educator and software practitioner in Vancouver. I’ve been a faculty member at the British Columbia Institute of Technology since December 2023, teaching programming and software systems and leading the Industry Sponsored Student Projects capstone course.

Teaching

I like the hands-on parts of teaching: writing code, talking through decisions with students, and trying different ways to explain something. I also build and experiment with tools for my classes. Working on those tools keeps me close to software development and gives me a way to rethink how I prepare and share course materials.

Industry experience

In industry, I enjoyed working with design and product colleagues to turn an idea into something we could try, refine, and eventually release. I worked across browser interfaces and the services behind them, building interactive learning activities, configurable data tools, and payment integrations.

My involvement often continued beyond the first implementation: working through architectural choices, reviewing code, and supporting the software in production. I also helped an early-stage company decide on its technical direction and build its initial engineering team.

Companies I’ve worked with Guavus · SMART Technologies · Localintel · Microsoft · Replayz

Research & publications

My research began in robotics at the University of Tehran. I explored how an air-hockey robot could learn to shoot accurately from a few examples, adjusting to the table’s friction and bounce. I worked on both the learning algorithm and the physical system, using computer vision to track the puck.

At the University of Calgary, I turned to how people communicate movement when they’re apart. I explored touch feedback for remote exercise and built HappyFeet, a shared virtual floor where dance partners could see each other’s foot movements. The work included the software connecting the two participants: MiddleMan relayed their data and could record and replay it with the original timing.
