About Teaching Projects

I teach computer science, build software, and try to stay curious about both.

These days, most of my work happens in the classroom. At the British Columbia Institute of Technology in Vancouver, I teach web development and software systems, including leading the Industry Sponsored Student Projects capstone course at the Downtown Campus. Teaching keeps me close to a beginner’s mindset: questioning assumptions, staying patient with uncertainty, and remembering that the obvious is only obvious in hindsight.

Before teaching, I spent seven years in software engineering, moving between R&D, rapid proofs of concept, and full-stack product development. I enjoy the whole arc: finding the right question, building the first rough version, shaping the architecture, and carrying the work into production alongside design and product teams.

Away from the classroom, I stay close to Persian poetry and music and play the tar. I also spend a lot of time wandering Vancouver with my toddler daughter, usually at her pace.

Now Faculty at BCIT · Fall 2026: COMP 1510 & the ISSP capstone program.
