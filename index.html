About Projects Writing

I teach computer science, and I keep ending up where people and machines have to take each other seriously.

These days that means the classroom: I'm on the faculty of Computer Information Technology at the British Columbia Institute of Technology in Vancouver, where I teach web development and software systems, and supervise student teams building real software for real sponsors.

Before teaching there was a decade of industry — payment infrastructure at Microsoft, civic data products at Localintel, learning activities at SMART Technologies — and before that, graduate school, where I taught a robot to play air hockey and gave remote dance partners a shared floor to stand on.

Off hours I stay close to Persian poetry — I spent a year as VP of the Calgary Persian Library, running book clubs and poetry-reading nights.

Now Faculty at BCIT · teaching COMP 1800, COMP 2510 & COMP 7855 · leading the ISSP capstone program.
