Hesam Alizadeh

I’m Hesam, an educator and software practitioner. I’m a faculty member at BCIT in Vancouver, where I teach programming and software systems, lead the Industry Sponsored Student Projects capstone course, and build tools for my classes.

I like staying hands-on: writing code, developing teaching tools, and experimenting with how I use them in the classroom. My experience in research and software development shapes how I teach, especially the attention I give to questioning assumptions, trying ideas out, and revising them as we learn.
