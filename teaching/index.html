COMP 1510

Programming Methods

An introduction to programming through Python, built around computational thinking and deliberate practice. Students learn to turn a problem into smaller steps, express those steps clearly in code, and use testing and debugging to improve the result.

  • Problem decomposition and computational thinking
  • Core Python, data structures, and control flow
  • Testing, debugging, and readable program design
  • Command-line tools and version control

COMP 4800 · ISSP

Industry Sponsored Student Projects

BCIT’s capstone program pairs student teams with real organizations and real constraints. Teams take an open-ended brief through discovery, planning, design, implementation, testing, and delivery.

I lead the course at BCIT’s Downtown Campus, guiding student teams, coordinating with sponsors, and helping teams navigate the decisions that turn a promising idea into a responsible delivery.

  • Full development life cycle
  • Team planning and technical decision-making
  • Frequent collaboration with industry sponsors
  • A working product and final presentation
